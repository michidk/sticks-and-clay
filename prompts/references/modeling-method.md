## Reference-led construction
Before the first build, write a compact working plan: the defining silhouette features, a few useful dimension ratios, important negative spaces, and structural parts in attachment/overlap order. Reuse and update this plan on revisions. Ground dimensions in the approved design; distinguish measured, estimated and inferred values. Perspective images do not establish exact orthographic measurements. Do not distort geometry to compensate for an unmatched reference camera. Record unseen sides as inferred, and resolve reference conflicts using the approved design and primary reference.

For each major part, choose a construction method that can express its contours: an extruded profile, lofted cross-sections, an edited mesh surface, a curve with varying radius, or a sculpted/remeshed volume. Primitives can start the blockout. Detail and subdivision cannot rescue an unsuitable underlying shape. Separate parts where real assembly, shading or movement requires it; continuous surfaces need deliberate transitions, not intersecting placeholder volumes.

Apply the relevant construction references below, not every technique to every asset. The current modeling pass controls scope: blockout stops at primary forms; refinement resolves construction; materials and polish develop the requested finish. Do not generate textures during geometry passes.

For each revision, identify the visible defect, likely cause, intended edit and features to preserve. Distinguish geometry, normals, UV and material failures before editing. Inspect the affected view and another angle after the change; avoid fixing a front silhouette at the expense of side volume. If repeated feedback names the same unresolved defect, reconsider the camera assumptions, topology or representation. Replace the failing part's construction method when necessary instead of repeating small parameter changes. Stay within the assigned pass and review limits.

Include the chosen methods, important inferred dimensions, actual before/after changes and unresolved issues in the existing final summary. Report only comparisons you actually performed; if previous imagery is unavailable, do not claim a verified improvement.
