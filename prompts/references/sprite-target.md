# Sprite output contract (takes precedence over ordinary texture requirements)

This job delivers sprites from the authored 3D model. The fixed spriteTarget parameters are in PARAMS; finish_asset automatically renders that target. Inspect preview_sprites after drafts and sprites after final export. The final pixel size, camera, palette, directions and motion determine visual acceptance. Do not judge only the beauty renders.

Intentional matte color regions are valid for this target. Use finish_asset(material_mode="poly") when those regions meet the design; the ordinary requirement to generate and bind image textures does not apply unless the brief explicitly requires a textured GLB too. Do not generate noisy textures just to satisfy the ordinary model workflow. Preserve useful 3D construction and animation.

Develop silhouette and negative space at native size: at 32 to 64 pixels a detail thinner than about one pixel vanishes or flickers between frames. Where the approved style allows, thicken such details, separate neighbouring color regions by value as well as hue (the palette reduction merges similar colors), and prefer few distinct materials with roughness around 0.6 to 0.9 over fine textures and reflections. Do not impose oversized heads, saturation, or nonmetallic surfaces on every design; the approved proportions win. Geometry-stage temporary materials remain valid.

Motion: animate limbs by rotating them around joint pivots: parent each limb to an empty placed at its joint (hip, knee, shoulder, elbow) and key the empty's rotation, so the limb swings from the joint instead of sliding. Use the requested animation clip and one consistent cycle convention: a looping range ends on the repeat of its first pose (key the first pose again at frame_end), and the renderer drops that terminal sample, so no frame appears twice in the sheet. Do not also stop one step before the repeat, which would drop a real pose. For one-shot motion the endpoint is included. Preserve contact, pivots, and hierarchy.

Every cell shares one ground pivot, so keep the model standing on z = 0 in every pose; the sheet records the per-direction baseline, a value range and a pixel-size check for each cell in sprite-checks.json. Review native size before enlarged cells. Compare every delivered direction for consistent proportions, attachments, handedness and palette. Inspect contact/extreme poses and last-to-first transition for popping, disappearance and clipping. Cite direction and source frame in findings. Technical topology warnings alone do not reject intentionally sprite-only hidden geometry, but explicit source-model requirements still apply. Do not approve unseen cells from a few selected poses.
