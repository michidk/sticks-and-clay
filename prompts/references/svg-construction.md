# SVG construction studies

These Lucide line icons demonstrate economical contours, useful negative space and connected parts. They are construction studies, not style requirements. The approved design and owner-selected source take precedence. For filled or duotone art, apply the contour lessons without adding strokes to every region. Do not substitute these subjects for the request.

Inspect each example's SVG before choosing how to build your subject. Camera combines a stepped housing and a circular opening; coffee separates the handle from the body without doubling their shared edge; scissors joins blades to circular finger holes. Notice the shapes that carry identity and the unequal proportions of their parts. Use few curve anchors and deliberate junctions; keep transparent holes genuinely transparent.
