## Deliberate UV and texture placement
Only for material/polish work or complete textured builds. Explicit poly mode uses colors without image textures.

- Plan a compact part-to-material-to-atlas-region mapping before requesting imagery. Each region needs a semantic purpose, orientation and repeat scale. Avoid mapping a whole composite image across every part. Large repeating surfaces can use tiles or trims; focal markings need deliberate placement. Use the existing generate_texture tool and assigned output directory.
- Select unwrap methods by surface shape. Put seams on hidden edges or logical construction boundaries. Cylindrical branches need lengthwise flow; panel strips need consistent orientation. Inspect a temporary checker on curved surfaces, junctions and thin strips to diagnose stretch and inconsistent density; remove the diagnostic material before delivery.
- Keep comparable parts at comparable texel density. For a mapped surface length L meters spanning u normalized UV units in a texture R pixels wide, density along that direction is approximately R*u/L pixels per meter. Use it as a diagnostic, not an invented resolution quota. Check padding and cross-region bleeding at reduced image size, not only full resolution. Do not mirror directional text or asymmetric markings.
- Connect base color in sRGB and normal/data textures in Non-Color. Ensure companion maps share the same region layout and UV transform, or explicitly use the correct alternative UV set. Generate flat material imagery without directional lighting; inspect grain direction and motif size on the model, not just the image.
- Derive normal detail from intended physical relief, not indiscriminately from color boundaries. Use a proper tangent-space Normal Map node with the expected convention. If baking, establish final topology/triangulation first and avoid projection onto neighboring parts. Do not connect an arbitrary generated RGB picture as a normal map.
- Inspect seams, stretching and normal strength after the final export. Preserve quiet surfaces where appropriate. Record which images are actually connected to exported materials, including reused images; a saved atlas that is never bound does not complete texturing.
