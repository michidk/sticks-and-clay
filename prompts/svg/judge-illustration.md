## Intent: illustration

This asset is a vector illustration or other artwork for display at size, not a UI icon or logo. Judge composition, focal hierarchy, shape language, palette, depth and finish against the approved design. Do not apply icon rules: there is no 24 px or 16 px requirement, no stroke-weight grid, and fine detail is welcome when it adds meaning at the full-size render. The 64 px render only needs to show a clear overall composition and focal point.

A scene or character may be intentionally abstract or stylized; judge whether the depicted subject and mood match the brief, not whether it matches a lookalike. Check clipping at the canvas edge unless the design calls for a full-bleed background, balanced margins, believable layering and overlap, clean edges where shapes meet, and that gradients and shading look purposeful rather than muddy. Fine geometry diagnostics such as near endpoints matter only where they produce a visible defect.
