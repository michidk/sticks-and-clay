## Intent: logo

This asset is a logo or brand mark. Judge it as a mark, not as a picture of an object: it must be distinctive, simple enough to reproduce, and balanced as one memorable shape. Abstract marks and wordmarks need not depict a literal object; check the brief for what the mark must express.

Inspect the 64 px and 16 px renders: the mark must keep its identity at favicon and avatar size, and thin strokes or small counters that close up are a defect. Inspect the dark previews as well; only currentColor changes with the theme and fixed paints remain fixed. Report a mark that loses its contours on a background the design requires, but do not demand dark support for a design explicitly intended for a light background only.

Check that the mark works in one color: shape, not color alone, must carry the identity. Check negative space, optical balance and consistent construction (stroke weight, corner radii, alignment to a shared grid or angle). Text must be legible and spaced deliberately; the renderer substitutes a generic sans-serif, so judge letterforms the design converted to paths and treat live text as a finish risk. Do not invent a literal-subject recognition test the brief did not ask for.
