## Intent: icon

This asset is a UI icon or a member of an icon set. For icons intended for 24 px, also inspect the supplied 24 px and 16 px renders when those sizes are requested; do not demand illustration details survive at 24 px when that size was not requested. Fine detail that disappears is a defect only if it carries meaning.

Junction audit: trace each outline in the enlarged render and inspect every endpoint and junction, especially lids, rims, spouts and handles. Look for cap overshoots, doubled edges, tiny spurs and unintended straight closing segments. A recognizable subject does not excuse these defects. Visible unintended strokes or joins that distort the requested contour are major defects. Name the visible region or element id and the desired contour change; preserve intentional open contours.

When an independent unlabeled reading is supplied in the analysis, compare its visible features and alternative interpretations with the intended subject. It was produced without the brief. A mismatch is evidence to investigate, not an automatic name-matching failure. Identify the visible cue that resolves the ambiguity or report the missing/distorted cue as a major defect. Do not explain away an ambiguous silhouette merely because the supplied name makes it imaginable. Abstract marks and illustrations need not depict a single literal object. Judge recognition-critical details before decorative simplification. A clean, consistent grid does not compensate for a generic blocky shape or incorrect proportions.

For specialist objects, the unlabeled reader may not know the exact name. Compare the visible anatomy against the subject references. A portafilter's shallow basket, locking tabs and attached handle can establish its identity even if a general reader guesses a pan. Conversely, the requested name does not excuse a deep pan bowl or missing attachment. Explain which visible cues support or contradict the requested object. Treat the reader as independent evidence, not a naming veto.

Inspect supplied dark previews as well as light previews. Only currentColor changes with the theme; fixed paints remain fixed. Report lost meaningful contours on backgrounds required by the design. Do not demand dark support for a design explicitly intended for a light background only. During silhouette exploration, assess anatomy, proportion and negative space; do not demand decorative finish. Human selection is required before refining a candidate.
