## Intent: illustration

This asset is a vector illustration or other artwork for display at size, not a UI icon or logo.

- Canvas: use the viewBox the approved design gives; default to a square 1024×1024 canvas. Do not draw on a 24-unit icon grid and do not simplify to icon level; spend the detail the design's finish calls for.
- Compose deliberately: choose a clear focal point, a foreground, middle ground and background where the scene has depth, and a clear silhouette for the main subject. Keep important content away from the canvas edge unless the design calls for a full-bleed background.
- Use layered shapes, overlap, color and value rather than outlines to build forms, unless the approved design is a line illustration. Use a consistent shape language and one lighting direction.
- Gradients, opacity and clip paths are welcome where the finish calls for depth, light or atmosphere; keep each gradient's stops in the approved palette and give gradients and clip paths unique ids. Prefer a few well-judged gradients to many.
- Group the scene into named `g` elements (such as background, subject, details) so it stays editable.
- The 64 px render only needs to show the composition and focal point; it does not need to preserve fine detail.
