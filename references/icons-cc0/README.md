# CC0 coffee references

Original files, with pinned sources and SHA-256 digests in [sources.json](sources.json).

- Maki cafe: economical solid silhouette and transparent handle opening on a 15-unit canvas. [Full CC0 dedication](maki-LICENSE.txt).
- Temaki coffee bean: a continuous outer contour and an open curved central seam. [Full CC0 dedication](temaki-LICENSE.txt).
- Espresso Coffee Gear, TheHolyCessna553: [original photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Espresso_Coffee_Gear.jpg). The central diagonal portafilter is the anatomy reference. Ignore the surrounding pitcher, tamper and scale. Original Wikimedia license metadata is retained in [espresso-gear-metadata.json](espresso-gear-metadata.json).

The icons are construction studies, not portafilter depictions. The photograph supplies subject anatomy. None prescribes a style that overrides an approved design. Lucide studies elsewhere in the repository retain their separate ISC/MIT licenses; they are not CC0.
